PROBLEM 6: LINK BUDGET

Question:
Calculate the received power using the given link-budget parameters.

Given:
Transmit power = 20 dBm
Transmit antenna gain = 5 dBi
Receive antenna gain = 3 dBi
Total link losses = 10 dB

Formula:
Received Power (dBm) = Transmit Power (dBm)
+ Transmit Antenna Gain (dBi)
+ Receive Antenna Gain (dBi)
- Total Link Losses (dB)

Calculation:
Pr = 20 + 5 + 3 - 10
Pr = 18 dBm

Final Answer:
Received power = 18 dBm

Verification Note:
The calculation assumes the listed gains and losses account for the link conditions and that all relevant losses are included.
