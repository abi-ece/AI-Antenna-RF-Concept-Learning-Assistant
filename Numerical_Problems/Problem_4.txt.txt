PROBLEM 4: VSWR

Question:
An RF system has a voltage reflection coefficient magnitude of 0.2. Calculate the VSWR.

Given:
|Γ| = 0.2

Formula:
VSWR = (1 + |Γ|) / (1 - |Γ|)

Calculation:
VSWR = (1 + 0.2) / (1 - 0.2)
VSWR = 1.2 / 0.8
VSWR = 1.5

Final Answer:
VSWR = 1.5:1

Verification Note:
The reflection coefficient magnitude is less than 1, so the calculated VSWR is finite and greater than or equal to 1.
