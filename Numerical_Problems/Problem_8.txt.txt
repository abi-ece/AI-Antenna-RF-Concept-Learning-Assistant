PROBLEM 8: REFLECTION COEFFICIENT

Question:
An RF system has a VSWR of 3:1. Calculate the magnitude of the voltage reflection coefficient.

Given:
VSWR = 3

Formula:
|Γ| = (VSWR - 1) / (VSWR + 1)

Calculation:
|Γ| = (3 - 1) / (3 + 1)
|Γ| = 2 / 4
|Γ| = 0.5

Final Answer:
Magnitude of voltage reflection coefficient = 0.5

Verification Note:
This is the inverse of the VSWR relationship for a passive transmission line with reflection coefficient magnitude less than 1.
