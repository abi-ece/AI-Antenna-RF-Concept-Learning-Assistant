PROBLEM 3: IMPEDANCE MATCHING

Question:
A transmission line has a characteristic impedance of 50 ohms and a load impedance of 75 ohms. Calculate the voltage reflection coefficient.

Given:
Z0 = 50 ohms
ZL = 75 ohms

Formula:
Γ = (ZL - Z0) / (ZL + Z0)

Calculation:
Γ = (75 - 50) / (75 + 50)
Γ = 25 / 125
Γ = 0.2

Final Answer:
Voltage reflection coefficient, Γ = 0.2
Magnitude of reflection coefficient = 0.2

Verification Note:
The calculation assumes a real, positive characteristic impedance and a purely resistive load. The reflected voltage-wave amplitude is 20% of the incident amplitude.
