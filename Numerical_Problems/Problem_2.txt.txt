PROBLEM 2: ANTENNA GAIN

Question:
An antenna has a directivity of 8 (linear) and an efficiency of 80%. Calculate its linear gain and gain in dBi.

Given:
Directivity, D = 8
Efficiency, η = 80% = 0.8

Formula 1:
Linear gain, G = η × D

Calculation:
G = 0.8 × 8
G = 6.4

Formula 2:
Gain in dBi = 10 log10(G)

Calculation:
Gain in dBi = 10 log10(6.4)
Gain ≈ 8.06 dBi

Final Answers:
Linear gain = 6.4
Gain ≈ 8.06 dBi

Verification Note:
Efficiency and directivity were given in linear form. The logarithmic conversion uses base 10.
