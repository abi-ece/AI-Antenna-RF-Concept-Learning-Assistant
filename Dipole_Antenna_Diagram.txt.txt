DIPOLE ANTENNA DIAGRAM

1. Definition

A dipole antenna consists of two conductive elements separated by a small gap at the centre.

2. Structure

Left Arm -------- Feed Point -------- Right Arm
                       |
                       |
                 Coaxial Feed Line

3. Antenna Length

Total length of a half-wave dipole = λ/2

Length of each arm = λ/4

4. Wavelength Formula

λ = c/f

Where:
λ = Wavelength in metres
c = Speed of light = 3 × 10⁸ m/s
f = Frequency in hertz (Hz)

5. Numerical Example

Given:
Frequency, f = 300 MHz = 300 × 10⁶ Hz

Wavelength:

λ = c/f

λ = (3 × 10⁸)/(300 × 10⁶)

λ = 1 m

Half-wave dipole length:

L = λ/2

L = 1/2 = 0.5 m

6. Result

Wavelength = 1 m

Approximate half-wave dipole length = 0.5 m

7. Note

The actual antenna length may be slightly shorter depending on the antenna design and operating conditions.
