PROBLEM 1: DIPOLE ANTENNA WAVELENGTH

Question:
An antenna operates at a frequency of 100 MHz. Calculate:
1. Wavelength
2. Approximate half-wave dipole length

Given:
Frequency, f = 100 MHz = 100 × 10^6 Hz
Speed of light, c = 3 × 10^8 m/s

Formula 1:
Wavelength = c / f

Calculation:
λ = (3 × 10^8) / (100 × 10^6)
λ = 3 m

Formula 2:
Half-wave dipole length ≈ λ / 2

Calculation:
L ≈ 3 / 2
L ≈ 1.5 m

Final Answers:
Wavelength = 3 m
Ideal half-wave dipole length ≈ 1.5 m

Verification Note:
These values are calculated using the speed of light and the given frequency. A practical dipole antenna may have a slightly different resonant length.
