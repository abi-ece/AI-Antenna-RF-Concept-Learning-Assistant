PROBLEM 5: FREE-SPACE PATH LOSS

Question:
A radio signal operates at 100 MHz and travels a distance of 2 km. Calculate the free-space path loss.

Given:
Frequency, f = 100 MHz
Distance, d = 2 km

Formula:
FSPL (dB) = 32.44 + 20 log10(f in MHz) + 20 log10(d in km)

Calculation:
FSPL = 32.44 + 20 log10(100) + 20 log10(2)
FSPL = 32.44 + 40 + 6.02
FSPL ≈ 78.46 dB

Final Answer:
Free-space path loss ≈ 78.46 dB

Verification Note:
The formula uses frequency in MHz and distance in km. It assumes ideal free-space propagation and does not include other system losses.
