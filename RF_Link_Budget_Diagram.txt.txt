RF LINK BUDGET DIAGRAM

1. Definition

An RF link budget calculates the received signal power by considering transmitter power, antenna gains, and system losses.

3. Link Budget Formula

Received Power (dBm) = Transmitted Power (dBm)
                       + Transmitter Antenna Gain (dBi)
                       + Receiver Antenna Gain (dBi)
                       - Total System Losses (dB)

4. Numerical Example

Given:
Transmitted Power = 20 dBm
Transmitter Antenna Gain = 5 dBi
Receiver Antenna Gain = 3 dBi
Total System Losses = 10 dB

Calculation:

Received Power = 20 + 5 + 3 - 10

Received Power = 18 dBm

Result:
Received signal power = 18 dBm

5. Important Note

This example uses the stated total system losses. In a real RF link, losses may include free-space path loss, cable loss, and other losses. Do not count the same loss twice.
