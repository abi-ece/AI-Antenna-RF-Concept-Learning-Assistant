PROBLEM 7: WAVELENGTH AND HALF-WAVE DIPOLE

Question:
An antenna operates at 300 MHz. Calculate the wavelength and approximate half-wave dipole length.

Given:
Frequency, f = 300 MHz = 300 × 10^6 Hz
Speed of light, c = 3 × 10^8 m/s

Formula 1:
Wavelength = c / f

Calculation:
λ = (3 × 10^8) / (300 × 10^6)
λ = 1 m

Formula 2:
Half-wave dipole length ≈ λ / 2

Calculation:
L ≈ 1 / 2
L ≈ 0.5 m

Final Answers:
Wavelength = 1 m
Ideal half-wave dipole length ≈ 0.5 m

Verification Note:
The half-wave length is an ideal approximation. A practical resonant dipole may be slightly shorter depending on its construction and surroundings.
