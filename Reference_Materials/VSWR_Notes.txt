# Topic 4: VSWR

1. Definition

VSWR stands for Voltage Standing Wave Ratio. It describes the relationship between the maximum and minimum voltage amplitudes of a standing wave on a transmission line.

2. Why Is It Important?

VSWR helps us understand impedance matching and the amount of signal reflection in an RF system.

3. Important Formula

VSWR = (1 + |Γ|) / (1 - |Γ|)

Where:
VSWR = Voltage Standing Wave Ratio
Γ = Voltage reflection coefficient
|Γ| = Magnitude of the reflection coefficient

4. Perfect Matching

When Γ = 0:

VSWR = 1

A VSWR of 1:1 indicates a perfectly matched load under the assumed conditions.
