# Topic 2: Antenna Gain

1. Definition

Antenna gain describes how effectively an antenna directs radio-frequency energy in a particular direction compared with a reference antenna.

2. Directivity

Directivity describes how concentrated the antenna's radiation is in a particular direction.

3. Efficiency

Antenna efficiency indicates how effectively the antenna converts accepted input power into radiated power.

4. Important Formula

Linear gain:

G = ηD

Gain in dBi:

G(dBi) = 10 log10(G)

Where:
G = Linear antenna gain
η = Antenna efficiency (linear)
D = Directivity (linear)
