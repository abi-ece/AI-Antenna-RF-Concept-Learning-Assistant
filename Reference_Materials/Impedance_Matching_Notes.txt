# Topic 3: Impedance Matching

1. Definition

Impedance matching is the process of adjusting the impedance between a source and a load to achieve the desired power transfer and reduce unwanted reflections.

2. Why Is It Needed?

- To reduce signal reflections.
- To improve power transfer under the intended operating conditions.
- To help RF systems operate effectively.

3. Important Formula

Reflection coefficient:

Γ = (ZL - Z0) / (ZL + Z0)

Where:
Γ = Voltage reflection coefficient
ZL = Load impedance
Z0 = Characteristic impedance of the transmission line

For a purely resistive load and real characteristic impedance, this formula applies directly. For complex impedances, use the same expression with complex values.

4. Perfect Matching

When ZL = Z0, the reflection coefficient Γ = 0, meaning there are no reflections at the load under the assumed conditions.
