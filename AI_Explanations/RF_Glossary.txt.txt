AI ANTENNA AND RF GLOSSARY

1. Antenna
A device that transmits or receives electromagnetic waves.

2. Dipole Antenna
An antenna consisting of two conductive elements.

3. Wavelength
The distance between corresponding points of a wave, represented by λ.

4. Frequency
The number of wave cycles per second, measured in hertz (Hz).

5. Antenna Gain
A measure of how effectively an antenna directs radio energy in a particular direction compared with a reference antenna.

6. Directivity
A measure of how concentrated an antenna's radiation is in a particular direction.

7. Impedance
The opposition a circuit presents to alternating current, measured in ohms (Ω).

8. Impedance Matching
Making source, transmission-line and load impedances compatible to achieve the intended power transfer and reduce reflections.

9. Reflection Coefficient
A quantity represented by Γ that describes the reflected voltage wave relative to the incident voltage wave.

10. VSWR
Voltage Standing Wave Ratio; the ratio of maximum to minimum voltage amplitude on a standing wave.

11. Link Budget
A calculation of transmitted power, antenna gains and link losses to estimate received power.

12. Free-Space Path Loss
The loss associated with radio-wave propagation through ideal free space.

13. RF
Radio Frequency; electromagnetic frequencies used for radio communication.

14. dB
A logarithmic unit used to express ratios of power or amplitude.

15. dBm
A power level expressed in decibels relative to 1 milliwatt.

16. dBi
Antenna gain expressed in decibels relative to an ideal isotropic radiator.

17. Characteristic Impedance
The impedance associated with a travelling wave on a transmission line, commonly represented by Z0.

18. Load Impedance
The impedance presented by the device connected to the output of a transmission line, represented by ZL.

19. Antenna Efficiency
The fraction of accepted input power that is radiated by an antenna.

20. Electromagnetic Wave
A wave consisting of coupled electric and magnetic fields that can carry energy through space.
