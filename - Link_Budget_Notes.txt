# Topic 5: Link Budget

1. Definition

A link budget is a calculation used to estimate the received signal power in a communication system by accounting for transmitter power, antenna gains and signal losses.

2. Why Is It Needed?

- To estimate the received signal power.
- To understand the effect of distance and frequency.
- To check whether a wireless communication link may work under specified conditions.

3. Basic Formula

Received Power (dBm) = Transmit Power (dBm)
+ Transmit Antenna Gain (dBi)
+ Receive Antenna Gain (dBi)
- Total Link Losses (dB)

4. Free-Space Path Loss

FSPL (dB) = 32.44
+ 20 log10(frequency in MHz)
+ 20 log10(distance in km)

This form uses frequency in MHz and distance in kilometres.

5. Important Note

The basic link-budget equation must include all relevant gains and losses. The free-space path-loss model assumes an ideal unobstructed propagation path and may not represent real-world conditions accurately.
