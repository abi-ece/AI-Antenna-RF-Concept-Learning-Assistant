# Topic 1: Dipole Antenna

**1. Definition**

A dipole antenna is an antenna consisting of two conductive elements that transmit or receive electromagnetic waves.

**2. Working Principle**

When alternating current flows through the antenna, it produces electromagnetic radiation. The antenna can also receive electromagnetic waves and convert them into electrical signals.

**3. Half-Wave Dipole**

A half-wave dipole is an antenna whose total length is approximately half the wavelength of the operating frequency.

**4. Important Formula**

Wavelength:

λ = c / f

Where:
- λ = Wavelength in metres (m)
- c = Speed of light in vacuum (approximately 3 × 10⁸ m/s)
- f = Frequency in hertz (Hz)

Approximate half-wave dipole length:

L ≈ λ / 2
